08 Product Backlog

## Purpose

The Product Backlog serves as the single source of truth for CareerOS development.

It defines the product vision, business goals, user journeys, feature priorities, and release planning for the entire platform.

Every feature, GitHub issue, sprint, and pull request should originate from this document to ensure the product evolves in a structured, consistent, and measurable way.

The backlog is a living document and will evolve as CareerOS grows.

## Product Goals

CareerOS aims to become the world's leading open-source Career Operating System by helping professionals continuously understand, improve, and achieve their career goals.

The primary goals of CareerOS are:

### 1. Provide Career Clarity

Help users clearly understand:

- Where they are today
- Where they want to go
- What skills they are missing
- What actions they should take next

---

### 2. Deliver Personalized Career Guidance

Generate personalized recommendations based on each user's:

- Skills
- Experience
- Projects
- Certifications
- Career goals
- Learning progress

No two users should receive identical career roadmaps.

---

### 3. Track Career Growth Continuously

CareerOS should evolve alongside the user's career by continuously updating:

- Skills
- Career readiness
- Progress
- Projects
- Certifications
- Recommendations

Career development is continuous, not a one-time assessment.

---

### 4. Provide Explainable Career Intelligence

Every score, recommendation, and insight must include a clear explanation supported by observable evidence.

Users should always understand:

- Why a recommendation was made
- Why their score changed
- Which actions will have the greatest impact

---

### 5. Help Users Make Better Career Decisions

CareerOS should reduce uncertainty by helping users answer questions such as:

- What should I learn next?
- Which certification provides the highest impact?
- Which project should I build?
- Am I ready for my target role?
- Which skills should I prioritize?

---

### 6. Create an Open Career Platform

CareerOS will be built as an open-source platform that is:

- Transparent
- Extensible
- Community-driven
- Vendor-neutral

The platform should enable contributors to build new integrations, features, and career intelligence capabilities over time.

### 7. Measure Career Progress

CareerOS should continuously measure professional growth over time rather than providing one-time assessments.

Users should be able to visualize:

- Skill progression
- Career readiness trends
- Completed milestones
- Learning consistency
- Goal completion

Success is measured not by the number of features CareerOS provides, but by the number of better career decisions it helps users make.

## Product Principles

Every feature developed for CareerOS must follow these principles.

### 1. Career First

Every feature should help users make better career decisions.

If a feature does not improve career outcomes, it does not belong in CareerOS.

---

### 2. Personalization by Default

Career recommendations should adapt to each user's goals, skills, experience, and learning journey.

No two users should receive identical recommendations.

---

### 3. Explainability Over Mystery

Every recommendation, score, and insight must include a clear explanation.

Users should always understand why CareerOS reached a conclusion.

---

### 4. Evidence-Based Intelligence

CareerOS should rely on observable evidence such as projects, certifications, GitHub activity, experience, and completed learning rather than assumptions.

---

### 5. Continuous Growth

Career development never ends.

CareerOS should evolve alongside the user's career and continuously update recommendations as new information becomes available.

---

### 6. Privacy by Design

Users own their career data.

CareerOS should request only the information necessary to provide value and give users control over what they connect, store, and share.

---

### 7. Open and Extensible

CareerOS should remain open source, community-driven, modular, and easy to extend through plugins and integrations.

---

### 8. Actionable Guidance

Every recommendation should tell users what to do next.

CareerOS should reduce uncertainty by turning insights into clear actions.

## Target Users

CareerOS is designed for professionals who want structured, data-driven guidance to achieve their career goals.

### Primary Users (MVP)

#### 1. Students

Individuals preparing for internships or their first full-time technical role.

Needs:

- Career direction
- Learning roadmap
- Skill tracking
- Project recommendations
- Interview preparation

---

#### 2. Early Career Professionals (0–5 Years)

Software engineers and IT professionals looking to grow into specialized technical roles.

Examples:

- Software Engineer
- Cloud Engineer
- DevOps Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- Data Engineer
- Cybersecurity Engineer

Needs:

- Career progression
- Skill gap analysis
- Career readiness
- Certification planning
- Personalized roadmaps

---

#### 3. Career Switchers

Professionals transitioning into technology or changing technical domains.

Examples:

- Mechanical Engineer → Software Engineer
- Support Engineer → DevOps Engineer
- Developer → Cloud Engineer

Needs:

- Structured transition plans
- Missing skill identification
- Learning priorities
- Progress tracking

---

### Secondary Users (Future Releases)

- Experienced Professionals
- Engineering Managers
- Career Coaches
- Recruiters
- Educational Institutions

Support for these users will be introduced in future releases after the MVP is complete.

## MVP Scope

The goal of CareerOS v0.1 is to validate the core product vision by helping users understand their current career position, identify skill gaps, generate personalized learning roadmaps, and continuously track professional growth.

The MVP focuses on solving one primary problem:

> "Help users understand where they are today, what they should learn next, and how close they are to achieving their target role."

### The MVP will include:

### Career Profile

- User profile
- Current role
- Target role
- Experience
- Skills
- Certifications
- Projects

---

### Career Intelligence

- Career readiness score
- Skill gap analysis
- Explainable recommendations
- Personalized career roadmap

---

### Progress Tracking

- Learning progress
- Completed milestones
- Career growth timeline
- Career readiness history

---

### Project Management

- Project portfolio
- Certification tracker
- Learning tracker

---

### Dashboard

- Career overview
- Progress visualization
- Recommended next actions
- Current priorities

---

### Basic AI Assistance

The MVP includes AI-assisted career guidance for:

- Skill recommendations
- Learning suggestions
- Career roadmap generation

AI will support decision-making rather than replace user decisions.
